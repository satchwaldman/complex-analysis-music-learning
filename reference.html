Minimum-viable complex analysis

You do not need a full course to understand the explicit formula — about a third of one. Full rigor needs the whole thing. In order:

  1. Holomorphic functions, Cauchy–Riemann, conformality — what “analytic” buys you (the 3Blue1Brown framing)
  2. Cauchy's integral theorem & the residue theorem — the workhorse; non-negotiable
  3. Analytic continuation & uniqueness — why ζ extends past σ>1, and why uniquely
  4. The argument principle — counts zeros; underlies N(T)
  5. Meromorphic functions, poles, logarithmic derivatives — you need −ζ′/ζ
  6. Hadamard product / factorization — the one genuinely heavier prerequisite; the bridge zeros→primes
  7. Functional equations — the ξ(s)=ξ(1−s) symmetry

For an EE who's done contour integration via inverse Laplace / Z-transforms, items 1–5 are a few weeks; item 6 is the real new investment.

From −ζ′/ζ to the zero waves (sketch)

  • Why ψ not π: with von Mangoldt Λ(n) = log p on prime powers, −ζ′(s)/ζ(s) = Σ Λ(n) n−s (log-derivative of the Euler product). Clean series, smooth main term ψ(x)~x.
  • Perron + contour shift: ψ(x) = (1/2πi)∫ (−ζ′/ζ)(s) xs/s ds; shifting left collects residues.
  • Residues: pole at s=1 → x (main term); s=0 → −ln2π; each nontrivial ρ → −xρ/ρ; trivial zeros → −½ln(1−x−2).
  • The wave: xρ/ρ with ρ=½+iγ gives x½eiγ ln x/ρ — an oscillation of frequency γ in u = ln x. That is the “spectrum.” See it assemble in the Explicit-Formula module.

The DSP analogy — how far it goes, where it misleads

Legitimate

The explicit formula is a spectral decomposition; primes ⟷ zeros is a genuine Fourier duality (rigorous as the Weil explicit formula, a trace formula). Resynthesis from the “spectrum” {γ} is the right mental model.

Misleading

The Dirichlet series does not converge on the line — ζ there is defined only by continuation. The zero-sum is conditionally convergent (pair ρ with ρ̄, truncate symmetrically). Self-adjointness (Hilbert–Pólya) is conjectural, not a given.

The counter-demo makes the first misleading point concrete: the naive partial sums at σ=½ never settle.

The research angle — two genuinely open questions

(a) Why the 1/√n weighting? σ=½ forces harmonic n to be weighted n−½. This choice is mathematical/aesthetic, not psychoacoustically derived — Buch: “chosen just for convenience.” No refereed pre-2026 work validates it against Plomp–Levelt / Sethares roughness data. Tractable, DSP-friendly: compare σ ∈ {½, ¾, 1, …} against empirical consonance rankings. Try it with the Explorer's σ-toggle.

(b) Composite “double-counting” — bug or timbre model? ζ sums over all integers, so composites (4, 6, 8, 9, …) are scored repeatedly, unlike prime-limit RTT. Is that arbitrary over-weighting, or exactly right for real harmonic timbres whose partials are all integers? An interference model naturally sums over all partial pairs — which could justify the composite counting. The audio pane's partials slider is the apparatus for probing this.

Honest size estimate: a strong paper or a thesis chapter, not a full PhD, and not numerology. The convergent-region identity is real math; RTT is rigorous; Guillet 2026 shows a refereed venue accepts the material. Proving the correspondence rigorously on the line is a much larger, harder project — keep it as a stated open problem, build the empirical/tool contribution instead.

Reading path (intuition → rigor)

  1. Mazur & Stein, Prime Numbers and the Riemann Hypothesis (CUP 2016) — start here. Builds the “Riemann spectrum” via Fourier analysis; Part I needs no calculus. Made for your background.
  2. 3Blue1Brown zeta video — watch alongside Part I for the continuation visual.
  3. Stein & Shakarchi, Complex Analysis (Princeton II) — the MVP subset above; read selectively (ζ, functional equation, PNT chapters).
  4. Edwards, Riemann's Zeta Function (Dover) — the deep dive; Riemann–Siegel, explicit formula, computations. Where you go for the truncation that fixes the arrow picture.
  5. Apostol, Analytic Number Theory — rigorous reference for Λ, Möbius, Dirichlet series. Look-up, not read-through.
  6. Keith Conrad's & Tao's expository notes — targeted clarifications (the 1+2+3…=−1/12 trap done right).
  7. Optional motivation: Derbyshire, Prime Obsession; du Sautoy, Music of the Primes (metaphor — hold at arm's length).

Staged plan with benchmarks

  • Weeks 1–3: Mazur–Stein Part I + 3B1B; reproduce the first zeros and |Z(2πx/ln2)| (done for you in the Explorer). Benchmark: explain why zero heights are “frequencies” and why the Dirichlet series doesn't converge at σ=½.
  • Weeks 4–8: the complex-analysis MVP from Stein–Shakarchi; derive the explicit formula's residue structure. Benchmark: reconstruct ψ0(x) and say where each term comes from.
  • Weeks 6–10: Xen Wiki zeta page + Battaglia derivation + Guillet 2026. Benchmark: state precisely which parts are convergent-region identities vs. continuation heuristics.
  • Weeks 10+: extend this explorer into an empirical study — does the ζ metric (and its σ-variants) predict human/consonance-model rankings better than TE badness or harmonic entropy? That attacks 1.4(a) and is the publishable core.

Crank attractors to avoid

  • “Elementary” proofs via the functional equation or a slick contradiction — arXiv is full of them.
  • Physics-flavored Hilbert–Pólya “I found the operator” claims (serious work exists; it does not prove RH).
  • Even eminent attempts fail publicly (Atiyah 2018; Stieltjes 1885). Short + extraordinary ⟹ almost always wrong.
  • The tuning material's own trap: don't leap from “great numerical fit” to “ζ explains consonance.” The zeta rating collapses the multidimensional character of temperaments — no substitute for investigating each.

Caveats on this tool's numerics

  • ζ(σ+it) is computed by Euler–Maclaurin (≈1e−7 across the plotted range); |Z| by the Riemann–Siegel main sum with the leading remainder. Accurate for exploration, not for research-grade zero-hunting.
  • Peak / octave-stretch readouts come from a local golden-section search near t = 2πN/ln2.
  • Guillet 2026's full text was inaccessible at the time of writing; claims about it rest on the abstract and a preprint figure. Verify the DOI and specifics before building research on top.
