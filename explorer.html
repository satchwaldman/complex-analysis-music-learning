The honest “sum of arrows.” On the critical line the infinite series Σ n−½−it diverges — you can't literally add the arrows. What actually works is the finite Riemann–Siegel main sum, cut off at n ≤ √(t/2π). Below you can watch those finitely many arrows line up at a peak.

The Riemann–Siegel main sum builds — arrows at this height. When they point the same way, |Z| spikes and that EDO is “good.”

How the three panes lock together. The tall |Z| peaks (zeta pane) sit at exactly the EDOs whose patent val has tiny weighted error (temperament pane), which are exactly the tunings whose approximations fall into the valleys of the dissonance curve (audio pane). Change σ and watch the peak list shift; add partials and watch the valleys move. You are looking at one fact from three sides.
